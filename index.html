A little clarity

Good questions.
Happy beginnings.

Everything you need to know before your child’s first hello.

Can my child join as a complete beginner?

Yes. Our sample beginner groups start with familiar topics, picture cards, songs and simple conversations. The trial helps identify a comfortable starting point.

How many children are in a class?

The demo programme is designed around small groups of up to eight children, grouped by age and language level.

Which languages can we choose?

English, Italian, Spanish and French are featured. Choose your preferred language in the trial form to explore the demo booking flow.

Are lessons online or in person?

This demo does not confirm a teaching location or delivery format. Share your preference in the trial form; a school would confirm available options before booking.

What if we miss a lesson?

Contact the school before the lesson where possible. Make-up classes, cancellations and any fees depend on the school’s confirmed policy.

Do we need to buy learning materials?

The demo lessons use games, stories and simple activities. Any required books, supplies or additional costs would be explained before enrolment.

Does the trial form make a real booking?

No. This is a demonstration form. It shows a booking summary locally and does not send your details or reserve a class.
